Mapillary-derived datasets — License & Attribution
===================================================

The datasets in this folder are processed from detections provided via the
Mapillary API (https://www.mapillary.com/developer/api-documentation) and are
based on user-contributed imagery and Mapillary's own processing.

    License:      CC BY-SA 4.0
                  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Per Mapillary's OpenStreetMap Wiki page
(https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Mapillary#License), these derived datasets
may be shared under the same license, provided that:

  - proper attribution is maintained ("© Mapillary"), and
  - any redistribution follows the CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

Files are named mapillary_<dataset>_DE-<STATE>_latest.parquet (one per German
federal state) and are refreshed automatically on a regular basis.

Processed and published by vizsim — https://data.vizsim.de
Source code: https://github.com/vizsim/mapillary_trafficsigns
